# /agrowise-content-plan

Turn a fuzzy "we should do more marketing" into a concrete, on-brand plan tied to the funnel and the calendar.

## Input

`$ARGUMENTS` — scope + constraints. E.g. "Q3 content calendar, focus nurseries" · "a 5-email nurture sequence for owners who tried the free Q&A" · "launch campaign for the paid copilot tier" · "content to run before Cultivate in July." If unscoped, default to a one-month content calendar across the beachhead segment.

## Steps

1. **Load the canon.** Read the `agrowise-brand` skill for the persona matrix, the wedge→platform stages, and the channel map (Cultivate, MANTS, Equip Expo, extension partnerships, trade media).

2. **Anchor to funnel stage.** Decide which GTM stage this serves: (1) free Q&A/blog wedge — build list + SEO/AI-search authority; (2) paid copilot conversion; (3) Canopy-connected tier; (4) landscape/PHC. The stage sets the goal and the CTA.

3. **Generate persona-tagged items.** For each item produce: working title · target persona · lead pain it hits · angle · channel · funnel stage · what sources it will need · rough effort. Lead with the reader's problem every time. Prefer topics where AgroWise can show real, cited specificity over generic SEO filler.

4. **Prioritize.** Rank by (impact on the current-stage goal) × (evidence we can source credibly) ÷ (effort). Call out the two or three items to do first and why. Note any item that needs primary research we don't have yet (e.g. the un-quantified landscape warranty-cost gap) — those double as proprietary, un-copyable content.

5. **Map to the calendar / channel.** Slot items against dates and channels, respecting the beachhead events (Cultivate July, MANTS January) and the in-person-first buying culture — content should feed trials and demos, not exist for its own sake.

6. **Return the plan** as a table plus a short "do these first" note. Offer to spin any item into a draft via `/agrowise-blog-draft`.

## Guardrails

- Every item is persona-tagged and problem-led; no generic "10 tips" filler.
- Tie to a funnel stage — content without a goal is noise.
- Flag items needing sources we don't yet have, rather than planning to fabricate them.
